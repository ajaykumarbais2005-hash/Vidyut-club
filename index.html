About Vidyut Club

MORE THAN A CLUB. A COMMUNITY OF INNOVATORS.

Vidyut Club is a student-driven technical and innovation community dedicated to developing practical skills, encouraging creativity, and building tomorrow's technology leaders.

01

Technical Workshops

Hands-on learning sessions covering programming, AI, web development, electronics, and emerging technologies.

02

Hackathons & Innovation

Collaborative competitions where students turn creative ideas into working solutions.

03

Technical Quizzes

Interactive challenges designed to improve technical knowledge, logical thinking, and problem-solving abilities.

04

Peer Learning

A collaborative environment where students share knowledge, build projects, and grow together.
